ABOUT

SFI St Paul's Cathedral Mission College

SFI St Paul's Cathedral Mission College has a long history of resistance and student organising. The unit works to spread awareness among students about their rights and to create a space where students can come together, discuss their concerns and take part in campus life.

At present, the unit organises a range of activities in college, including student clubs, study circles, cultural programmes and sports activities. This website brings together information about these activities and provides students with a simple way to stay connected with the unit.
